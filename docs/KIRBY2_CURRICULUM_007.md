The six strand names are presentation groups over the existing stable skills,
not new skill identities. A bounded, versioned clinic selector uses explicit
observations and misses, with no mastery or prerequisite-readiness inference.
The closed legacy scoring policies do not describe these new exercises; do not
insert new results under a legacy policy or reinterpret its learned projection.
The new policy remains UNVALIDATED_FOR_LEARNING_OUTCOMES.

Two conditions remain separate. The case clinic is untimed, with immutable
lead-in from actual C4 execution and declared fixed observation cuts. It measures
control instruction accuracy and evidence interpretation, not reaction speed.
Prepared instructions are labeled and never count as learner execution. Joined
practice retains the precursor to partial fills and cancellation. Valid mistaken
learner instructions reach the existing engine; grading never changes the fills.
The complete workflow launches the existing continuous Radar world and retains
its original saved evidence for review; open judgment receives no automatic
profit-based grade. Its session-like distribution remains the C6 bounded synthetic
rotation, not a claim about real-market opportunity frequencies.

Freeze a baseline → targeted rehearsal → unfamiliar matched-condition → delayed
retention protocol before revealing cases. A durable append-only local ledger
records selections, exposure, assistance, answers, aborts, failures and original
evidence. Fresh means a previously unexposed case, never an unfamiliar skill or
an unseen market model. Related exercise forms share lineage. A later local
clock permits retention after 24 hours; clock rollback refuses availability.
Local clock time is scheduling metadata, not secure elapsed-time attestation.

No scores pool assistance, familiarity, form or automation conditions. Missing
reaction time stays unavailable. No-opportunity, ambiguity, abort and system
failure retain their own denominators. No external reminders or automation.

Deviation record: all images prohibited by current user instruction. No visible
Qt, image-producing tests, worker contact, push, release/remote qualification or
human-learning claim. Use source review and protected non-image checks. Human
learning evidence remains NOT_EXERCISED. Stop before Chapter 8.
