# Synthetic learner protocol V1

This protocol measures performance on Kirby2 exercises. Its software can be
accepted without a participant completing it. Current actual human participation
and learning benefit: **NOT_EXERCISED**. It cannot establish profitability,
real-market transfer, or a causal benefit from training.

## Before the first answer

Open **Practice path**, then **Freeze learner protocol**. This saves the population,
conditions, policy and metrics before any results. One local ledger supports one
learner protocol: six baseline cases, focused rehearsal, six fresh cases and six
retention cases. The 64-attempt and 512-event budgets include all attempts and
failures. Keep the original ledger; replacing the data root starts an unrelated
record, not verified freshness for the same learner.

The six headings refer to existing skills: HOTKEY_ACCURACY, BOOK_READING,
SPREAD_DECISION, LATENCY_AWARENESS, PARTIAL_FILL_MANAGEMENT and SCRIPT_DISCIPLINE.
Joined responses may additionally observe POSITION_MANAGEMENT and CANCEL_TIMING.
These are narrow instruction/interpretation samples. They do not establish general
competence in every activity named by a skill. Canonical prerequisites are shown
as dependencies; these samples do not certify prerequisite readiness or feed the
closed legacy mastery estimator.

All clinic cases are **untimed**. The engine reconstructs a fixed precursor and
fixed subsequent observation intervals. Assessment cuts do not depend on hidden
future outcomes or on the learner's answer. They are not tests of reaction speed.
The orders marked PREPARATION are part of the exercise, never learner trades.
Mistaken valid learner orders execute with their real simulated consequences.
No feedback rewrites a fill to create success.

## Prespecified sequence

1. **Baseline:** use Recommended next for all six cases, without hints. Record
   every answer. An abort or system failure remains in the population; it does not
   disappear or silently become a successful attempt. Finish this phase only
   after all six cases are accounted for. A repeated or externally revealed case
   is labeled rehearsal and excluded from an unfamiliar comparison.
2. **Targeted rehearsal:** select the recommended weakness, or choose a strand
   manually. Start with Isolated, then Joined. Use Show answer when helpful; it
   records assistance. For pending exposure, the join is prepared submission →
   delivered partial fill → possible-position judgment → learner cancellation →
   delivered confirmation. Repeat keeps the exact recipe; another rehearsal
   selects a distinct seeded case. Neither is called a fresh assessment.
3. **Fresh:** finish the practice phase, then complete the six new reserved cases
   without hints. The same strand and condition recur with changed quantities,
   timing offsets and seeded depth. These are unfamiliar cases within known
   recipes, not new market families or validated difficulty equivalence. Report
   the actual quantities and observation conditions with results.
4. **Retention:** after finishing Fresh, the local schedule makes retention
   available 24 hours later. There is no reminder or background task. Finish the
   six reserved retention cases. The local wall clock is scheduling metadata,
   not secure proof of elapsed time. A clock rollback refuses new writes.
5. **Complete workflow:** separately launch Complete workflow · Radar. Observe,
   inspect, select or decline, arm, execute if warranted, then freeze and save.
   Save links the exact world authority and source to the enrollment. Close the
   world before opening the curriculum history. Review every candidate, quiet
   interval, action, outstanding commitment and fill. Early freeze is a partial
   sample; pause/overload/visibility history remains in the original. This source
   has no authenticated human-versus-script origin on each C6 action, so it earns
   no automatic manual-skill score. The C6 three-ticker, eight-second rotation is
   a separate synthetic session distribution, not a market-frequency model.

## Metrics and exclusions

Compare baseline, fresh and retention **within** strand, form, mode, familiarity,
pace, assistance and script conditions. Report correct decisions and eligible
opportunities, not only a percentage. No-opportunity and ambiguous evidence remain
separate. Prepared orders do not count as responses; responses marked SCRIPT
cannot increase the manual denominator. Desktop responses are HUMAN_DECLARED;
that label is an input-origin declaration, not proof that a human used the device.

Completed attempts contribute to the progress accuracy fraction. Aborts, system
failures, and partial answers stay in history with distinct counts. Joined
supporting-skill observations remain linked to their original question instead of
inflating the primary skill. Original evidence opens from each history row. No
reaction latency is inferred from simulation time. No P&L, streak or trade count
is used to rank mastery.

One case per strand per assessment phase is a very small descriptive sample.
Do not pool mismatched conditions to obtain a flattering score. Do not rerun a
revealed case and call it new. Record unfamiliar-case exclusions and inspect the
raw sources before interpreting any before/after change. If rehearsal improves
but new-case performance does not, revise the task, explanation or sampling
before multiplying content. A stronger study needs additional independently
reviewed case families and matched populations; real-market transfer needs a
separately authorized observed-data or paper environment.

## Recovery and interpretation

Every enrollment and response is saved atomically before the desktop announces
it. If presentation fails, choose **Refresh / recover**; a response may already
be durable and must not be submitted twice. Closing the application during an
idle clinic preserves the pending attempt. Resume it or explicitly Abort; its
exposure remains recorded. An unfinished Radar world cannot resume from the
curriculum enrollment; use the existing saved world evidence or abort the
unattached enrollment.

The engineering acceptance packet contains scripted software checks, not a
participant dataset. Any later human results must be reported separately with
sample conditions, exclusions and the limits above.
